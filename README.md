# CALDERA WIND: Volcanic Archipelago Bush-Jet Sorties — 3D Flight Physics, Lit Cockpit MFDs, 4 Distinct Missions & Graded Landings

Play **Caldera Wind** offline in any modern desktop browser (`node serve.ts`, then open `http://127.0.0.1:8080`). Pilot the twin-tail STOL utility jet **KW-4 Kestrel** across the volcanic Kestrel Isles across four distinct sorties with energy-based aerodynamics, fuel/hull management, chase and full 3D lit cockpit views, live PAPI runway approach lights, and procedural Web Audio soundscape.

## Features
- **True Energy Flight Model**: Quaternion attitude dynamics, lift/induced-drag turn bleed, stall buffet & recovery, slotted flaps (`0°/15°/30°`), speedbrake, afterburner fuel burn, and responsive speed-scaled keyboard + optional mouse-yoke steering.
- **Chase & Full 3D Lit Cockpit Views (`C` / `V`)**: Switch seamlessly between spring-damped chase camera and a sculpted 3D cockpit featuring live Primary Flight Display (PFD), Engine/Hull MFD, rotating Tactical Navigation Radar, collimated HUD glass, and moving control stick & throttle quadrant.
- **4 Distinct Sorties**:
  1. **Caldera Ring Circuit** (Golden Hour) — Takeoff, 7 aerial ring gates over the suspension bridge & crater lagoon, and precision Runway 09 touchdown.
  2. **Basalt Canyon Slalom** (Alpine Midday) — High-speed gorge gates under crosswind pressure with tight fuel reserves and canyon shortcut decisions.
  3. **Monsoon Beacon Drop** (Amber Storm Dusk) — Deliver 3 parachute supply crates to smoke-marked outposts under 18 KT crosswind turbulence and recover on the offshore Carrier Barge or Main Airstrip.
  4. **Caldera Glide Recovery** (Crimson Sunset) — High-altitude emergency reserve-fuel dead-stick descent and graded flare onto Runway 09.
- **Readable Landings & Black-Box Crash Diagnostics**: Real-time touchdown vertical speed (`FPM`), bank/gear checks, tire smoke, landing grades (`S / A+ / B / C`), and clear cause-of-impact flight-recorder reports.

## Controls
- **Pitch / Roll**: `W/S` & `A/D` or Arrow Keys (optional Mouse Yoke toggle: `M`)
- **Yaw / Rudder**: `Q` / `E`
- **Throttle / Afterburner**: `Shift` / `Ctrl` (or `=` / `-` or Mouse Wheel)
- **Flaps**: `F` (`0° / 15° / 30°`) · **Landing Gear**: `G` · **Airbrake**: `B` (hold)
- **Supply Drop**: `Space` · **Camera (Chase / 3D Cockpit)**: `C` or `V`
- **Flight Assist**: `T` · **Switch Sortie**: `1`–`4` · **Pause**: `P` / `Esc` · **Restart**: `R`
